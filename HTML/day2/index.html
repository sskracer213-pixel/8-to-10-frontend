good morning guys tree is a large, woody plant that has a single main stem called a trunk. Trees have branches, leaves, roots, and often produce flowers, fruits, or seeds. Parts of a Tree Roots – Absorb water and nutrients from the soil and hold the tree firmly. Trunk – Supports the tree and transports water and food. Branches – Spread out from the trunk and hold the leaves. Leaves – Make food for the tree through photosynthesis. Flowers/Fruits – Help the tree reproduce by producing seeds. Importance of Trees 🌿 Produce oxygen. 🌍 Reduce air pollution. 🌞 Provide shade and keep the environment cool. 🐦 Offer shelter and food for birds and animals. 🍎 Give fruits, wood, medicines, and many useful products. 🌧 Help prevent soil erosion and support rainfall.
