WIKIPEDIA

Wikipedia is a free, multilingual, web-based encyclopedia written and maintained by a community of volunteer editors. Launched on January 15, 2001, by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger, it has grown into one of the largest and most widely read reference works in human history. It is hosted and funded by the Wikimedia Foundation, a non-profit organization funded primarily through public donations.
