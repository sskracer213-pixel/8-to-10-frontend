ZOHO is a software company that provides modern technology solutions for businesses. We create websites, mobile applications, and software that help customers grow their business Zoho is an Indian multinational technology company that develops cloud-based software for businesses. It provides applications for customer relationship management (CRM), accounting, human resources, email, project management, and many other business needs. Explanation Zoho Corporation was founded in 1996 by Sridhar Vembu and Tony Thomas. Its headquarters is in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Zoho offers more than 50 business applications that help organizations manage their daily operations efficiently. The company's products are used by businesses of all sizes, from startups to large enterprises, in many countries around the world.
