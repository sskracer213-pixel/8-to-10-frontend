ENGINEERING COLLEGE DEPARTMENTS

CSE

Computer Science and Engineering focuses on programming, software development, AI, and networking.

IT

Information Technology deals with software, databases, web technologies, and cloud computing.

ECE

Electronics and Communication Engineering studies communication systems and electronic devices.

EEE

Electrical and Electronics Engineering focuses on electrical machines, power systems, and electronics.

Mechanical

Mechanical Engineering deals with machine design, manufacturing, thermal engineering, and robotics.
