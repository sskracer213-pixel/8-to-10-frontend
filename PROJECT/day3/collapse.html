THE EPISTEMIC LESSON

The Real Lesson: The Fragility of Frictionless Optimization

“The problem is not that humans cannot live without technology. The problem is that modern society has been built around technology so deeply that removing it suddenly creates catastrophic disruption because we eradicated all analog redundancies.”

The Efficiency Trap

In pursuit of maximum quarterly margin, enterprise software replaced paper backups, local inventories, and manual bookkeeping with single-threaded cloud APIs.

The Antifragile Mandate

The goal cannot be to abandon technology. The goal must be to build antifragile systems: decentralized, memory-safe, offline-capable, and anchored by skilled human judgment.
